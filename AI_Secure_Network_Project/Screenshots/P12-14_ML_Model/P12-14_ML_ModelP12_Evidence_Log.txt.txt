PHASE 12-14 - ML MODEL (PYTORCH) - EVIDENCE LOG
Platform: Google Colab (CPU). Dataset: CIC-IDS2017 (cleaned), 2,520,751 flows.

Goal: detect attacks, especially NOVEL ones that signature rules (Suricata) miss.
Labels: Normal = 0, Attack = 1 (binary). Data imbalanced: 83% normal.
Features (6, chosen because Suricata flow logs provide the same):
  Destination Port, Flow Duration, Total Fwd Packets,
  Total Length of Fwd Packets, Flow Bytes/s, Flow Packets/s

Generalization split:
  Train/Test: Normal + DoS + DDoS + Port Scanning (80/20 split)
  Unseen (never in training): Brute Force + Web Attacks + Bots (13,241)

Model: MLP 6 -> 32 -> 16 -> 1 (ReLU), Adam lr 0.01, 100 epochs
  v1 plain loss:     Recall 46.57%  (missed half the attacks - imbalance)
  v2 weighted loss:  pos_weight 5.08 -> Recall 72.84%  (fixed)

Threshold tuning (v2):
  Thr  TestRecall  Precision  NoveltyDetect
  0.5    72.84%     54.00%      52.23%
  0.4    81.47%     48.42%      71.81%   <- CHOSEN
  0.3    91.97%     27.61%      72.79%   (precision collapses)

KEY RESULT: 71.81% of attack types never seen in training were detected.
